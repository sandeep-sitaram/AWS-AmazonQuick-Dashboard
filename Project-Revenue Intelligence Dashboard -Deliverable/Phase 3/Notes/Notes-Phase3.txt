

 Phase 3 Dashboard Notes
Overview*
The NovaTech Unified Insights Dashboard integrates CRM, Marketing, and Support datasets to provide unified, cross‑functional insights across revenue performance, campaign effectiveness, and support efficiency. By combining three operational datasets into a single analytical view, the dashboard enables leadership to identify trends, compare product performance, and evaluate customer experience metrics in one place.


Visuals Included

1. Deal Value by Product Category  
Shows total revenue contribution by product category.  
This helps leadership understand which product lines drive the highest deal value and where strategic investment may be most impactful.
2. Deal Duration Trend
Displays how long deals take to close over time.  
Negative durations were corrected using `abs()` to ensure accurate representation.  
This visual highlights sales cycle efficiency and identifies periods where deal closure slowed or accelerated.

3. Marketing ROI by Campaign 
Compares ROI across marketing campaigns.  
This visual identifies which campaigns generated positive returns and which underperformed, supporting better budget allocation and future campaign planning.

4. Support Tickets by Priority
Shows distribution of support tickets by urgency level.  
This helps support leadership understand workload pressure, resource needs, and customer pain points.

5. Support Efficiency Score by Product Area
Displays total support efficiency score across product areas.  
This highlights which product areas have the fastest and most effective support resolution, and where operational improvements may be needed.

6. Revenue vs Support Efficiency (Scatter Plot) 
Cross‑dataset insight combining CRM and Support data.  
Shows whether high‑value customers receive faster support.  
This visual demonstrates unified dataset functionality and provides leadership with a holistic view of customer value vs. customer experience.

Data Quality Adjustments

Negative Deal Duration Correction
Some deals had `deal_closed_date` earlier than `deal_created_date` due to data inconsistencies.  
To ensure accurate visualization, Deal Duration was corrected using:

abs(dateDiff({deal_closed_date}, {deal_created_date}, 'DD'))

Null Value Replacement**
Null values in categorical fields were replaced using `coalesce()` to improve readability and prevent blank labels:

Examples:

coalesce({product_category}, 'Unknown')
coalesce({campaign_name}, 'Not Provided')


Join Strategy
Left joins were used to preserve CRM as the anchor dataset and avoid losing records during dataset unification.

Filters Added
The dashboard includes four sheet‑level filters to enable interactive exploration:

Industry,Sales Region ,Campaign Channel,Priority

These filters allow leadership to slice insights by customer segment, region, marketing channel, and ticket urgency.

Purpose
The dashboard provides a unified operational view across NovaTech’s CRM, Marketing, and Support functions.  
It enables leadership to:

- Identify high‑value product categories  
- Monitor sales cycle efficiency  
- Evaluate marketing performance  
- Understand support workload distribution  
- Compare customer value vs. support experience  
- Make data‑driven decisions across departments  

This unified dashboard supports strategic planning, operational improvements, and customer‑centric decision‑making.
