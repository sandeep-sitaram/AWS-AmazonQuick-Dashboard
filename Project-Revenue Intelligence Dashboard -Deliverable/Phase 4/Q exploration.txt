# Q Exploration Log — Revenue Intelligence Dashboard

This log captures at least five Q queries across Marketing, Sales, and Support domains, with Q responses and dashboard verification.

---

## Entry 1 — Marketing: Campaign Performance

 Question asked:   
Which campaign source has the highest conversion rate?

 Q response summary:   
Q returns a bar chart showing conversion rate by campaign source (e.g., Email, Social, Search), with Email at the top.

 Dashboard verification:   
On the  Marketing Funnel  sheet, the “Conversion Rate by Campaign Source” visual shows Email as the highest conversion rate, matching Q’s result.

 Verdict:   
Accurate — Q correctly interprets “campaign source” and “conversion rate” using the Marketing dataset and Topics.
